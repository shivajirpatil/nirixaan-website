Market Timing

Why India,
why now

India's industrial inspection market is at an inflection point. Security and surveillance AI is well-served. Custom LabVIEW integrators exist. What doesn't exist: an India-native, software-first platform purpose-built for manufacturing quality AI — combining drone-agnostic ingestion, multi-vertical inspection models, and repeatable deployment. Nirixaan owns that category.

01

Foreign vendors pulling back

Global consolidation among legacy inspection vendors is creating account uncertainty across Indian DISCOMs and infrastructure clients — a direct opening for a committed India-native partner.

02

No India-native platform

Existing Indian players either do security & surveillance AI or one-off LabVIEW integrations. Nobody owns manufacturing quality AI — precision defect detection, dimensional inspection, and asset health monitoring — as a repeatable software platform. That category is ours.

03

NDT market growth

India NDT market: ₹5,700 Cr → ₹15,600 Cr by 2033. Maharashtra/Pune belt is our home ground.
